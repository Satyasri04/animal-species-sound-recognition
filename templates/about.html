How SoundSpecies Works

Automated multi-class animal species recognition through acoustic signal processing and deep neural networks

⚙️ Processing Pipeline

1
Upload
Audio file loaded at 16kHz mono via Librosa
2
Extract
YAMNet generates 1024-dim embeddings
3
Classify
Deep CNN classifies into 12 species
4
Result
Species name and confidence displayed

🎯 What It Does

This application uses deep learning to identify animal species from their sounds. Upload a .wav audio recording, and the AI will analyze the acoustic features and predict which of 12 animal species produced the sound, along with a confidence score.

📂 Dataset

Trained on a curated subset of the ESC-50 dataset — 480 samples across 12 species. Each clip is 5 seconds at 44.1 kHz, downsampled to 16 kHz for processing.
